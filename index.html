Your stats, where you’re already looking.

Open Vantage on your desktop between games. Keep the overlay on while you play.

Match history

Browse recent matches with scoreboards, round results, and your performance on each map.

Rank progress

See your current rank, rating changes, and your trend over the season.

Session overlay

A compact overlay shows your win rate and averages for the session. Drag it anywhere and set a hotkey to hide it.

Agent and map breakdowns

Find out which agents and maps you win on, and where you fall short.

Built to stay within the rules.

Vantage is a stats tracker, not an advantage tool.

What the overlay shows

  • Your own match history, rank, and session stats
  • Information from completed matches
  • Data from the official Riot Games API

What Vantage never does

  • Read or modify the game’s memory or files
  • Reveal enemy positions, loadouts, or ultimates during a live match
  • Automate input or play for you

Questions

Is Vantage free?

Pricing hasn’t been decided. The core stats and overlay are planned to be free.

Will I get banned for using it?

Vantage doesn’t touch the game client. It uses the official Riot Games API and draws its own window above your game. You should still read Riot’s current policies for third-party tools.

Does the overlay work in fullscreen?

Overlays that draw over a game work best in borderless windowed mode. Exclusive fullscreen may hide them.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

Which regions are supported?

Region support depends on the Riot Games API. A list will appear here before launch.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. This page describes the planned behavior of Vantage and will be updated before launch.

What we collect

  • Your Riot ID and the match data the Riot Games API provides for it.
  • Settings you choose in the app, such as overlay position and hotkey.

How we use it

We use this data only to show you your own stats and to make the app work. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it’s stored

Settings and cached match data are stored on your computer. You can delete them by uninstalling the app or clearing its data folder.
